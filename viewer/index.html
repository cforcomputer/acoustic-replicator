What you are looking at

Printed frame (two halves) holds 18 identical boards; each board sits in a pocket and is held by four screws. 450 transducers (40 kHz) all face the centre.

Jet in the stand under the bottom port fires one droplet straight up through the light gate; anything that falls lands in the drip cup.

Three cameras in the upper ports, three red strobes in the lower ports, eleven 405 nm emitters for the cure flash.

Lift-out plate in the top port carries the build pin; the finished part comes out with it.

Print playback shows the cured surface computed by the landing model; the pale blobs are the most recent droplets. In-camera view renders what a machine camera sees: the cage is dark, lit only by the strobes and the violet cure flash. Physics test flights are full acoustic simulations, slowed down 50× and looped.

Every solid in this view is at its real size and is checked for overlaps, for being fastened to something, and for wires that end nowhere (hardware/*.json).
